        💡 Why your choices matter

        Two numbers decide how smog hits you: how much PM2.5 you breathe in and how much you add to the air. Shifting one hour of outdoor activity from the morning peak to the afternoon window avoids hundreds of micrograms of PM2.5 inhaled — and swapping a 10 km car commute for the metro saves about 1.1 kg of CO2 a day, roughly 5.5 kg a work week. The plan above puts a number on every choice, so you can see the trade-off instead of guessing.
